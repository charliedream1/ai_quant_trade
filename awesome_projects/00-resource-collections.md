## Awesome AI4Finance

A curated RL-in-finance list (MIT license). Source: [FinRL Blogs](https://github.com/AI4Finance-Foundation/FinRL-Blogs). Survey paper: [Recent advances in reinforcement learning in finance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.04553).

- **[WRDS](https://github.com/wharton/wrds)** ⭐ 96 — A Python data access library for academic usage.
