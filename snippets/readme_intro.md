**AI Quantitative Trading Bot** is a one-stop platform that takes you from
learning and simulation all the way to live trading — with runnable example
projects for every major strategy family.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-chart-line: __Local Quant Strategies__

    ---

    Rule-based (double MA, portfolio), ML / DL, reinforcement learning,
    GNN, high-frequency and alpha-mining examples with full tutorials.

-   :material-robot-outline: __LLM Applications__

    ---

    LLM-driven forecasting, sentiment analysis and research workflows,
    including SFT training pipelines you can reproduce locally.

-   :material-database-outline: __Data & Tooling__

    ---

    Market-data pipelines, factor libraries, stock monitoring and
    recommendation tools used in day-to-day trading.

-   :material-rocket-launch-outline: __Live Deployment__

    ---

    Simulation and live-trading templates supporting Python/C++,
    CPU/GPU and multiple brokers.

</div>

> Every `egs_*` project is self-contained: clone the repo, `pip install -r
> requirements.txt` inside the project directory, and follow its README.
> See the [Quick Start](https://github.com/charliedream1/ai_quant_trade#-quick-start)
> guide for details.
