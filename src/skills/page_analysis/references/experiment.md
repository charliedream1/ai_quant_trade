# Saved experiment and execution review

Identify the exact saved task/version, universe, dates, data revision, price
adjustment, execution timing and benchmark. Check whether train/validation/test
separation and original point-in-time availability were actually recorded.
Distinguish performance measurements, model estimates and missing validation.
Review return/drawdown conventions, turnover, slippage, fees, lot sizes, liquidity
constraints and cash/position reconciliation from supplied deterministic results.
For factors discuss coverage, lag alignment, IC distribution, stability, overlap,
multiple testing and selection bias. A high mean IC is not sufficient validation.
For compositions explain sleeves, cascades/votes, weight/risk constraints and
which saved run produced the observed outputs. Do not silently execute a preview.
Separate an accounting inconsistency from a strategy hypothesis or market risk.
Recommend targeted independent checks; do not certify code correctness, robustness
or profitability without the required ledger, source and validation evidence.
