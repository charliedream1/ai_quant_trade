# Read-only portfolio, candidate and ETF review

Preserve account identity, currency, valuation time and missing-price warnings.
Separate cash, marked holdings, floating P&L and realized ledger performance.
Identify concentration and common exposures without inventing correlation estimates.
For candidate lists compare declared criteria and source coverage; a screening
score does not prove investability or an out-of-sample advantage.
For ETFs distinguish product exposure, benchmark, fees, tracking difference,
tracking error, liquidity and spread. Only quantify a metric when the required
observations exist. Do not infer historical constituent membership from today's
universe. Preserve HK board-lot, currency and settlement limitations when relevant.
Use recorded fills and fee assumptions for execution commentary; a broker order
status is not a confirmed fill. Proposed checks are research observations and
must never become orders, risk-limit changes or account actions.
