# Market and policy news review

Identify original publisher, publication time, event time and affected geography.
Separate enacted policy, proposals, official guidance and attributed commentary.
Group duplicates without turning repeated coverage into independent confirmation.
Explain plausible transmission through rates, credit, demand, supply chains,
regulation or risk appetite, and list evidence that would disconfirm each path.
Distinguish domestic, overseas and cross-border effects using the selected filters.
Do not manufacture market reactions from headlines or infer unknown timestamps.
State unavailable original text, conflicting reports and missing price evidence.
Rank monitoring questions by relevance and uncertainty rather than predicting a
trade direction. Cite the supplied original observations for every factual claim.
